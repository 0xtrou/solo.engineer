## Goal
New `/crypto` terminal — clone infra terminal pattern. Aggregates launches (BT via Google News proxy + Airdrops.io + CoinGecko trending), L1/L2 chain blogs, news (CoinDesk/CoinTelegraph), market data (CoinGecko prices + DeFiLlama TVL). Per-article category scoring + per-source composite quality score. Live price tiles.

## Risks
- **CoinGecko rate limit**: free tier ~5-30/min. 3 endpoints per cycle, 10min revalidate = well under limit. Safe.
- **Google News proxy**: BT headlines via Google fetch — may lag/delay vs direct. Acceptable given BT is CF-walled.
- **JSON adapters for CoinGecko/DeFiLlama**: map to article shape with synthesized titles (e.g. "Bitcoin: $XX,XXX (+X%)"). Honest — shows real data, not fake news.
- **CategoryScores fork**: small duplication. Worth it for clean separation.
- **L2 coverage thin**: only Blast has RSS. Most L2s (Arbitrum/Optimism/Base/zkSync/Starknet) have no public RSS — would need scraping. Out of scope.

## Out of scope
- BitcoinTalk direct (CF-walled, Google proxy instead)
- L2 blogs without RSS (Arbitrum/Optimism/Base/etc. — 8 outlets dead/no-feed)
- CryptoPanic (auth-gated), The Block (CF-walled), CoinMarketCal (CF-walled)
- On-chain data (would need RPC/GraphQL to chain nodes)
- Wallet/DEX integration
